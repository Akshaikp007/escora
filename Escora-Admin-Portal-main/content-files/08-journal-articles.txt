Excerpt:
Discovering the unhurried rhythm of life along the meandering canals of
Alleppey, where time stands still and nature reclaims the soul.

Body:
The engine cuts out somewhere past the third bend, and Rajan — who has piloted
kettuvallam on the Vembanad for thirty-one years — says nothing.
He doesn't need to. The silence does all the explaining.

"To travel by water in Kerala is to understand that the destination was never
the point. The point was always the canal itself."

"Thirty years," Rajan says, apropos of nothing. "Same lake. Different every day."

A walk through the cobblestone streets of Fort Kochi, uncovering tales of spice

Body:

"Five empires have left their signatures on this peninsula. None of them quite

you can say about it.
